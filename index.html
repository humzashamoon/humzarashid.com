Clinical Trials Research Administrator

UConn Health Clinical Trial Office
Why it matters

A cancer trial generates thousands of data questions from its sponsor. When nobody clearly owns a decision, they pile up, staff burn out, and patients wait longer to start treatment.

What I do
  • Built and piloted OCTANE, which gives every decision an owner and an escalation path across regulatory, clinical, and data teams.
  • Sort sponsor queries by source and type to find what causes the backlog.
  • Track query burden, resolution time, protocol deviations, and time from consent to first treatment.

Roots

It started in Vehari.

My grandfather was the first Western-trained physician in Vehari, Pakistan. He built the area's first X-ray and a tuberculosis prevention program, and he chose to stay and serve his hometown.

As a child I sat beside him in his clinic. He showed me that a physician can care for one patient and still strengthen the health of a whole community. That is the physician I'm training to be.

MPH Practicum · Dec 2026 – Jan 2027

HPV vaccine trust in Lahore.

Pakistan launched HPV vaccination for girls 9–14 in September 2025. Coverage in Punjab is 47.6%, and the Institute of Public Health names hesitancy as the main barrier.

I'm partnering with IPH's awareness team to support sessions with families and frontline health workers, and to turn their feedback into recommendations for future campaigns.

I found this project through two months of cold outreach across Pakistan. When I asked Dean Afzal what her community needed most, she named HPV vaccine hesitancy.
